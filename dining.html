From Our Kitchen

Three Houses, One Pantry

Every dish at the Cascadian begins within fifty miles — foraged in the forests, fished from the Sound, or grown in the orchard behind the kitchen.

The Hearth

Dinner · Tasting Menu · Reservations

A nine-course exploration of the Pacific Northwest, anchored by a wood-fired hearth at the center of the dining room. The chef's menu changes weekly with the season.

Cedar & Salt

Breakfast · Lunch · Casual

Our all-day room with a long copper bar and a view of the Sound. Smoked salmon at dawn, oysters at midday, and a wine list drawn from the small producers of the Yakima Valley.

The Lobby Bar

Cocktails · Light Plates · Evenings

An after-hours room defined by a quiet jazz program, an extensive American whiskey selection, and a short menu of small plates from The Hearth.

The Orchard Table

Private Dining · By Arrangement

A single long table set among the apple trees, available for parties of eight to twenty. Menus are designed in conversation with the chef and the gardener.
