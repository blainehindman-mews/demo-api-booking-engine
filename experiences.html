Experiences

From sunrise on the water to alpine evenings on the mountain, a quiet list of the things we love about this corner of the country.

On the Water

Sunrise on the Sound

A guided ferry crossing as Seattle wakes behind you — pour-over coffee, a quiet captain, and the city skyline at first light.

In the Mountains

Mt. Rainier Trailheads

Step from the lobby onto trails that climb to alpine meadows. Day packs, a packed lunch from Cedar & Salt, and a guide if you'd like one.

At Altitude

The Lake Lookout

A private drive into the North Cascades National Park, ending at the lookout above the lake. Best in early morning, when the water is still glass.

In the Forest

Old Growth Walk

A naturalist-led morning walk through the cedar grove behind the hotel — suitable for all paces. Returns to the orchard for tea.

In the Cellar

Wines of the Pacific Northwest

An evening tasting led by our sommelier, walking through the small-production wines of Washington, Oregon, and British Columbia.

By Air

Seaplane to the San Juans

Depart from Lake Union, land in Friday Harbor. A day of orcas, oysters, and the slow rhythm of the islands.
