Our Story

A House Built
of Stone & Cedar.

For more than a century, travelers have come to the Cascadian to slow down. Our halls are warmed by Pacific Northwest timber; our windows open to the same vistas that drew the first settlers to this corner of the country.

From sunrise meditations beside the lake to evenings shaped by the chef's seasonal table, the Cascadian invites you to step softly into something older — and a little wilder — than the world you left behind.
